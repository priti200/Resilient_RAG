2) Why each column was removed
------------------------------
The removal decisions come directly from analysis_results/removed_features.csv and the corresponding entries in analysis_results/cleaning_log.csv.

High-missingness columns:
- DstJitAct, DstJitter, SrcJitAct: These three columns had disproportionate missingness and were considered non-essential in the final model-ready set.

Potential leakage / context columns:
- Ssaddr, Sdaddr: source/destination identifiers may encode host or traffic identity.
- RunTime, Dur, Offset: these fields carry timing or temporal context that is not expected to be available in real-time detection.

Target leakage columns:
- BinaryLabel, SubCategoryLabel: both are label-derived fields and therefore must not be used as model inputs.
